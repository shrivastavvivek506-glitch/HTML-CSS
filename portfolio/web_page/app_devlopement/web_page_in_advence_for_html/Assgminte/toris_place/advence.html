Top 10 Tourist Places in India

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal in Agra is a symbol of love built by Shah Jahan and is one of the Seven Wonders of the World.

Jaipur

Jaipur, the Pink City, is famous for its forts, palaces, and vibrant culture.

Goa

Goa is known for its beautiful beaches, nightlife, and Portuguese heritage.

Kerala

Kerala is famous for its backwaters, greenery, and houseboat experiences.

Ladakh

Ladakh is known for its breathtaking mountains, monasteries, and adventure tourism.

Varanasi

Varanasi is one of the oldest cities in the world, famous for its ghats and spiritual atmosphere.

Mysore

Mysore is known for its grand palace and rich cultural heritage.

Shimla

Shimla is a popular hill station known for its scenic beauty and cool climate.

Darjeeling

Darjeeling is famous for tea gardens and stunning views of the Himalayas.

Rishikesh

Rishikesh is known as the Yoga capital of the world and for adventure sports like river rafting.
