Tourist Place in india

1. Taj mahal

The Taj Mahal is a world-famous white marble mausoleum built by Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

2. Qutub minar

Qutub Minar is a UNESCO World Heritage Site located in Delhi, India. It is the tallest brick minaret in the world, standing at 73 meters (240 feet) tall.

3. Jaipur(hawa mahal)

Hawa Mahal, also known as the "Palace of Winds," is a stunning five-story palace located in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. It was built in 1799 by Maharaja Sawai Pratap Singh and is renowned for its unique architecture and intricate latticework.

4. Red fort

The Red Fort is a historic fort built by Shah Jahan in 1648 as the main residence of Mughal emperors.

5. Goa beach

The Goa beaches are famous for their golden sands, clear waters, and vibrant nightlife. Popular beaches like Baga Beach and Calangute Beach attract tourists from all over the world.

6. Kerala Backwaters

The Kerala Backwaters are a network of brackish waterways and lakes located in the state of Kerala, India. They are known for their scenic beauty and are a popular destination for houseboat cruises.

7. Mysore Palace

Mysore Palace is a historic palace located in Mysore, Karnataka, India. It is known for its stunning architecture and rich cultural heritage.

8. Haridwar

Haridwar is a holy city located in the state of Uttarakhand, India. It is one of the seven sacred cities in Hinduism and is known for its spiritual significance and beautiful landscapes.

9. Varanasi

Varanasi, also known as Benares or Kashi, is a city located in the state of Uttar Pradesh, India. It is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world and is considered a spiritual and cultural hub.

10. Agra Fort

The Agra Fort is a UNESCO World Heritage Site located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. It was built by the Mughal emperor Akbar and is known for its impressive architecture and historical significance.
